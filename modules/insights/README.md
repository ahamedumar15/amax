# insights (change 026)

The weekly agent briefing, projects at risk, opportunities from your people's skills, talent growth, market position, world trends to act on, and the recurring agent jobs that keep it all current. People read and decide; agents write the insights.

- **Owner:** `/modules/insights/` in `.github/CODEOWNERS`
- **Screen:** `/insights` (Work; Admin, Leadership and PM). It leads with "This week's briefing" (three points, each with its suggestion, sources, confidence and date, "Discuss with amax" and "Show evidence"), then Projects at risk (Delivery, Commercial and People tabs), Opportunities from your skills, Talent growth (headcount and AI-skills chart with a screen-reader table, attrition, promoted, certifications), Market position with the peer bars and the live pipeline, World trends (Take on, Watch, Skip) and Recurring agent work. "Next 90 days" and "Next 12 months" are read-only horizons; "Board pack" exports the screen as CSV (formula cells neutralised). There are no forms: actions and the job switches open chat, and every card, row, stat, bar and trend is pinned.
- **Sources (AC-1):** every insight has at least one source, a label and an evidence link (an amax path or an https URL), and a confidence and a date, shown as "From … ; medium confidence; Oct 12". `record_insight` refuses an insight without a source, a deferred constraint trigger refuses one at commit, and reads drop any that has none.
- **Live and stored (AC-2):** what other modules publish is read live through their contracts, as the viewer: headcount, joiners and leavers (attrition: leavers in 12 months ÷ the average headcount on the first of each month) from hr `listPeople`; certifications valid and expiring in 90 days from learning, for the people the viewer may see; the open pipeline and closed deals from presales (with `presales:read`); utilisation from reports. What contracts don't publish yet (velocity, due dates and margin; the skills matrix; promotions; RFP debriefs and client conversations; the 12-month win rate) are insight records, written by an agent job or `record_insight`. Each record is scoped to a legal entity (or the tenant) and may need a further permission (a People risk needs `hr:read`).
- **Agents (029 schedules, T3):** the weekly strategy briefing (`insights.briefing`, Mondays 07:00) thinks over the current insights in 006's untrusted-input mode for the person in `set_briefing_reader`: insights go to the model only as data, it gets no tool but `propose_suggestion` (kind `insights.briefing-point`), and each point keeps only sources it was given; a point with none is dropped. The daily project risk scan (`insights.risk-scan`, 06:00) reads allocations, approved leave and logged hours as each project's manager; the weekly skills-to-opportunity match (`insights.skills-match`, Mondays 06:00) reads who rolls off, their certifications and the open pipeline. All write through `record_insight`; a newer insight with the same key supersedes the older.
- **Recurring agent work (AC-3):** every installed module's agent jobs (the schedules behind its 006 suggestion kinds, the inbox agent's "Teams and email scan" among them) and insights' own. The switch asks amax to `set_schedule_enabled` (Settings, Workflows' permission); others see On or Paused.
- **Contracts** (`contracts.ts`): `KINDS`, `CONFIDENCE`, `HORIZONS`, `JOBS`, `EVENTS` and payload types, `InsightRef`, `currentInsights(tx, viewer, { kind, horizon })`.
- **Events:** `insights.insight.recorded` and `insights.insight.dismissed` (ids, kind, counts and who; never the text).
- **MCP tools:** read `get_insights` (format `board-pack-csv`), `get_insight`, `get_insight_job`; write `record_insight`, `dismiss_insight`, `set_briefing_reader`. Chat intent: record an insight.
- **Retention (032):** an insight and its sources two years from the day it is as of, then deleted.
- **Depends on** hr, delivery, time and presales. **Integrates with** allocations, leave, finance, it-access, recruitment, learning and reports, each optional: without them their figures are left out (and said so) and the agents skip what they can't read.
- **Fixtures:** the demo's pinned today (Oct 16), Maya as the briefing reader, and the mock's briefing, risks, opportunities, talent records, market position and trends, all fictional and each with sources.
- **Tests:** `pnpm --filter @amax/insights test` (DB tests need `DATABASE_MIGRATE_URL`; the model is always a fake); screen: `pnpm --filter @amax/web build && pnpm --filter @amax/insights e2e`.
