# compliance (change 036)

Policies and their acknowledgements, the risk, incident and supplier registers, the daily audit-chain check and signed evidence bundles per ISO 27001 control, for ISO 27001 and SOC 2 audits.

- **Owner:** `/modules/compliance/` in `.github/CODEOWNERS` (IT lane).
- **Screen:** `/compliance`, in the Operations group for Admin, IT Admin, HR and Leadership. It replaces 055's "Coming soon" entry (switch the module off and the entry comes back). It leads with what needs attention: policy acknowledgement, breach decisions due, open risks and the last audit-chain check; then incidents, policies, each control's evidence, open risks and the suppliers and subprocessors. Every write is an Ask amax button or a chat intent; every stat, card, row and line has a pin.
- **Contracts** (`contracts.ts`), version 1: `pendingPolicies(tx, reader, personId)` (onboarding can ask what a person still has to acknowledge), `latestChainChecks(tx, reader, limit?)`, `listSubprocessors(tx, reader)`, the status, class and control lists, `EVENTS` and the payload types. A reader is a viewer (with `compliance:read`, or the person themselves for their own policies) or a system context core issued with `contextFor.job`; a hand-built system context fails closed.
- **Events:** `compliance.policy.published`, `compliance.policy.acknowledgement_requested` (one per person in scope with a sign-in), `compliance.policy.acknowledged`, `compliance.risk.raised`, `compliance.risk.changed`, `compliance.incident.reported`, `compliance.incident.changed`, `compliance.incident.breach_flagged`, `compliance.incident.deadline_due`, `compliance.supplier.changed`, `compliance.chain.checked`, `compliance.evidence.exported`. Every payload field is declared (035).
- **MCP tools** (`mcp.ts`): read `get_compliance_overview`, `get_incident`, `get_risk`, `get_supplier`, `get_policy`, `get_evidence_bundle`, `list_risks`, `list_suppliers`, `list_audit_chain_checks`, `get_my_policies`; write `report_incident`, `update_incident`, `add_risk`, `update_risk`, `add_supplier`, `update_supplier`, `publish_policy`, `acknowledge_policy`, `export_evidence`, `check_audit_chain`.
- **Chat intents** (`chat.ts`): report incident, new risk.
- **Permissions:** `compliance:read` and `compliance:manage` for Admin, IT Admin and HR (Leadership reads); everyone holds `compliance:report` (report an incident) and `compliance:acknowledge` (their own policies).
- **Policies:** one row per version. Publishing a version with the same key asks everyone in its scope (every active person, or one legal entity's) to acknowledge it again, through a 030 notification each.
- **Breaches** (`packs.ts`): an incident involving personal data is a possible breach. Its decision deadline starts when it is flagged, from its legal entity's country pack: Australia 30 days to assess (Privacy Act 1988, Part IIIC), Sri Lanka 72 hours (Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022), 72 hours with no pack. Confirm these against the regulators' current guidance; they are not legal advice. The owner and Admins are told when it is flagged; the `compliance.breach-reminders` workflow (daily, 07:00 UTC) reminds the owner every day in the last week until a decision is recorded.
- **Audit chain:** the `compliance.audit-chain` workflow (daily, 06:00 UTC) verifies the 003 hash chain and records how far it verified, or the event where it broke with that row's action and resource. A broken chain tells Admins and fails the run.
- **Evidence:** `export_evidence` writes one dated bundle per control (A.5.1 policy acknowledgements, A.5.18 access reviews, A.5.24 incidents, A.6.3 training, A.8.15 audit-chain checks), each with a SHA-256 digest and an HMAC-SHA256 signature under the audit key; `get_evidence_bundle` says whether it still verifies. Bundles hold counts, not the names of people still to acknowledge.
- **Suppliers:** the register's rows, plus the platform's own subprocessors read from 033's AI and data settings (the AI provider and its region, the database host, file storage and the browser speech service when voice is on), so the two screens can't disagree.
- **Integrates with** (optional): **learning** (024): A.6.3 lists the mandatory and compliance programmes through `@amax/learning/contracts`. Per-person completion isn't in learning's contract v1, so the control doesn't claim "184 of 184" as the mock does. Without learning (switched off, so its schema isn't migrated) the control states the gap. **it-access** (021) isn't on main yet: A.5.18 says "Needs IT access (021)" until it is.
- **Depends on:** hr (people in a policy's scope, through `listPeople`, `getPerson` and `personIdsForUser`).
- **Mock notes:** the mock's incident is due Oct 20; Australia's rule gives 30 days from Oct 16, so the demo's is due Nov 15. The mock's lede opens with the ISO 27001 audit date, which no register holds yet. The mock shows no risk or supplier list; the screen adds both below the mock's content (AC-3). Its "Report incident" button carries the spark of every chat intent, not the mock's plus.
- **Data:** `compliance.policy`, `compliance.acknowledgement` (pseudonymous ids only), `compliance.risk`, `compliance.incident`, `compliance.supplier`, `compliance.chain_check`, `compliance.evidence_bundle` (RLS on `tenant_id`); retention entries in `module.ts`. All demo records are fictional.
- **Tests:** `pnpm --filter @amax/compliance test` (DB tests need `DATABASE_MIGRATE_URL`); screen e2e: `pnpm --filter @amax/compliance e2e` after `pnpm --filter @amax/web build`.
