# helpdesk (change 022)

IT, HR and Facilities queues with response and resolution SLAs, tickets from chat, Teams and email, and a support agent that resolves only through approved actions. It replaces the external service desk.

- **Owner:** `/modules/helpdesk/` in `.github/CODEOWNERS`
- **Screen:** `/helpdesk` (Operations), for IT Admin, HR and Admin. It leads with the agent's solved share this month and the tickets close to breaching (a quarter of the target left) or past their SLA, then the queue tabs (IT, HR, Facilities; HR only for HR and Admin, Facilities with IT), "New ticket" (opens chat in `helpdesk.new-ticket`), four tiles (open and waiting on the requester, solved by the agent against last month, first response against the Normal target, satisfaction over 90 days), "Tickets per week" (solved by the agent vs by people, eight ISO weeks) and "Tickets by category" (this month), each with a screen-reader table, and the queue table (ticket, requester, category, priority, SLA left, handled by): open tickets and those closed in the last day, newest first, 8 at a time with "Show all". An empty queue says so, with New ticket. Every tile, chart, legend row, queue and ticket is pinned; there are no forms. A ticket's title and text are stored and shown as plain text; a source link is kept only when it is https to Outlook or Teams (or a reserved `.example` host).
- **Queues and SLAs (AC-1):** `helpdesk.queue` (IT `INC-`, HR `HR-`, Facilities `FAC-`) and `helpdesk.sla`: a response and a resolution target per queue and priority (low, normal, high, urgent), changed with `set_queue_sla` (IT Admin, Admin; only for a queue they work in). "SLA left" is worked out when read: the response target until someone responds (assigning or resolving counts), then the resolution target; paused while the ticket waits on the requester.
- **Tickets (AC-1, AC-3):** `create_ticket` from chat or MCP, and from an inbox find (025), which passes its channel (`teams`, `email`) and the link to the message. Anyone raises a ticket for themselves; someone who works in the queue can raise one for another person. `assign_ticket`, `resolve_ticket` (desk staff, in their own queues) and `rate_ticket` (the requester, once resolved) are audited services. Each ticket keeps its channel, source link, who raised it and who handles it (a person, or the support agent).
- **Signed in (AC-2):** a ticket is `signed_in` only when its requester raised it themselves in chat or Teams. Email never is.
- **The support agent (AC-2):** the `helpdesk.triage` workflow (029) runs every five minutes over new tickets. Each goes to the model through `think` from `@amax/agents` in 006's untrusted-input mode: the ticket is only `data`, the instructions are fixed, and no tool but `propose_suggestion` (kind `helpdesk.triage`) is offered. Its triage sets the category and priority. For an access request to a resource in the catalogue (`helpdesk.access_resource`, set with `set_access_resource`), from a signed-in requester, it makes the `helpdesk.access` suggestion, `sensitive`, which only that resource's owner can approve (`helpdesk:access:approve@own` with scope `ownerUserId`; Admin can too, as with every permission). Approval resolves the ticket as "Granted by support agent after <owner> approved" and emits `helpdesk.access.approved`; undo reopens it. Making the grant in 021 is in BACKLOG.
- **Credentials (AC-2):** a password, MFA or credential request goes to the IT queue as "Needs identity verification", whatever queue it was raised in, and never reaches the model. Resolving it needs a resolution that says how the identity was verified.
- **HR (AC-2):** HR tickets and the HR queue are read only by holders of `helpdesk:queue:hr` (HR, and Admin). Every read and write checks the queue; a requester always sees their own ticket through `get_ticket`.
- **Contracts** (`contracts.ts`): `QUEUES`, `PRIORITIES`, `CHANNELS`, `CATEGORIES`, `STATUSES`, `TRIAGE_KIND`, `ACCESS_KIND`, `SUPPORT_AGENT`, `TRIAGE_JOB`, `queueCounts(tx)` (open and waiting per queue, counts only), `EVENTS` and payload types.
- **Events:** `helpdesk.ticket.created`, `helpdesk.ticket.resolved`, `helpdesk.access.approved` (ids, queue and channel only). Handles onboarding's `onboarding.checklist.started`.
- **MCP tools:** read `get_helpdesk`, `get_ticket`; write `create_ticket`, `assign_ticket`, `resolve_ticket`, `rate_ticket`, `set_queue_sla`, `set_access_resource`. Chat intent: `helpdesk.new-ticket`.
- **Depends on** nothing but the platform (002, 003, 006, 029). **Integrates with** onboarding (013): a joiner's checklist opens one Hardware ticket "Laptop for new joiner, <first day>", raised by their manager; and hr (011): requester names come from hr's contract, else the user's name. The inbox agent's (025) ticket route lights up once `helpdesk.ticket` exists. Each is optional.
- **Retention (032):** tickets and their events are deleted seven years after the ticket closes; a rating is deleted with its ticket.
- **Fixtures** (fictional): the three queues and their targets, three repos with owners, the demo's pinned clock (Oct 16, 10:00 UTC) and the mock's figures: 27 open (9 waiting), 41% solved by the agent in October against 29% in September, first response 7 min against 30, 4.7 from 212 ratings, 163 October tickets by category, and INC-20431, INC-20429, INC-20427 and HR-1186. Facilities is empty.
- **Tests:** `pnpm --filter @amax/helpdesk test` (DB tests need `DATABASE_MIGRATE_URL`; the model is always a fake); screen: `pnpm --filter @amax/web build && pnpm --filter @amax/helpdesk e2e`.
