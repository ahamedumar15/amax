# expenses (change 018)

Expense claims and reimbursements in place of the email-and-spreadsheet process. People claim through chat; their manager, then Finance, approve; payment goes through payroll or by bank.

- **Owner:** `/modules/expenses/` in `.github/CODEOWNERS`
- **Screen:** `/expenses` ("Expenses"). The mock has no Expenses section (018's Mock Delta: no screen of its own); the screen exists because the rail lists every module's root, and it reuses the mock's tiles and decision-table patterns. The lede says what waits for the viewer's decision, then the Waiting for you, Approved to pay and Over the limit tiles, "Needs your decision" (Approve and Reject are Ask amax buttons), "Claims" ("Claim an expense" opens chat with the `expenses.claim` intent) and "Categories and limits". Every tile, card and row is pinned; there are no forms.
- **Claims (AC-1):** a claim has a category (per legal entity, with a limit per claim and the amount over which a receipt is needed), the day, merchant, note, amount and currency. Over the receipt threshold a claim needs a receipt; over the limit it is flagged to the approvers, not refused. It goes to the claimant's manager (hr reporting line; straight to Finance when there's none), then to Finance in the entity, who may not be the person who approved it as manager. Finance picks payroll (the default when payroll is installed: the claim joins this month's pay period) or bank, and records a bank payment with its reference. Rejecting takes a reason.
- **Receipts (AC-1, AC-2):** `add_receipt` takes a photo or PDF (base64, up to 8 MB) and, when the client has it, its text (a PDF's text layer or the device's OCR). The file goes to object storage through core's S3 adapter (`storageFromEnv`: `S3_ENDPOINT`, `S3_BUCKET`, …); without storage configured the tool says so. `get_expense_claim` gives a five-minute link to the receipt, never to someone who may not see a medical claim's detail.
- **Receipt agent (AC-2):** on `expenses.receipt.added` it reads the receipt's text (a parser first; the 033 AI gateway only when the parser can't read it and AI is on) and proposes merchant, day, amount, currency and category as an `expenses.receipt-claim` suggestion (006). Only the person who added the receipt may accept it, which files the claim; undo withdraws it while no manager has decided. The chat drawer has no file picker yet and `@amax/ai` takes text only, so photos without text get no suggestion; adding both is a platform change.
- **Payroll (AC-1):** `reimbursementsDue(tx, reader, { legalEntityId, period })` lists the approved claims Finance sent to a pay period, for payroll to add to net pay. When payroll emits `payroll.payslips.sent`, the expenses agent suggests marking that period's claims paid (`expenses.payroll-paid`); Finance accepts once they're on the payslips. Undo puts them back to approved.
- **Sensitive data (002):** a medical claim's merchant and note are `health`: the claimant and HR see them, the manager and Finance see the category and amount. Read tools declare them as `sensitive` so the AI gateway redacts them per the tenant's policy. Events carry no amount or merchant.
- **Contracts** (`contracts.ts`): `STATUSES`, `STATUS_LABELS`, `PAY_VIA`, `CLAIM_SENSITIVE`, `listClaimRefs(tx, reader, personId?)`, `reimbursementsDue(tx, reader, scope)`, `EVENTS` and payload types.
- **Events:** `expenses.receipt.added` (handled by its own receipt agent), `expenses.claim.submitted`, `expenses.claim.approved` (both steps; `status` says which), `expenses.claim.rejected`, `expenses.claim.paid`. Handles `payroll.payslips.sent`. Notifications: the approver chain hears of a new claim, Finance of one ready for it, the claimant of the decision.
- **MCP tools:** read `get_expenses`, `list_expense_claims`, `get_expense_claim`, `list_expense_categories`, `get_expense_category`; write `add_receipt`, `claim_expense`, `approve_expense`, `reject_expense`, `record_expense_payment`, `set_expense_limits`. Suggestions are accepted with 006's `accept_agent_suggestion`.
- **Permissions:** everyone: `expenses:read`, `expenses:claim`, `expenses:approve` (only for their reports' claims). Finance: also `expenses:review`, `expenses:approve-finance`, `expenses:pay`, `expenses:policy`. Leadership and Payroll: `expenses:review`. Admin: all, including `expenses:admin` (may take the manager step).
- **Depends on** hr (011) and agents (006). **Integrates with** payroll (014), optionally: without it, claims are paid by bank and `payVia: payroll` is refused.
- **Data:** `expenses.category`, `receipt`, `claim`, all with RLS on `tenant_id` and amounts `numeric(19,4)`. Retention: claims and receipts seven years from the claim, then anonymised.
- **Fixtures:** fictional. Categories for Lumen Labs Lanka (LKR: Travel 25,000, Meals 5,000, Equipment 60,000, Training 100,000, Medical 20,000) and Lumen Labs Pty (AUD: Travel 500, Meals 80). Claims EXP-0101 to EXP-0106: Kasun's taxi, Ayesha's team lunch (over the Meals limit) and Dinesh's medical claim wait for Maya; Nimal's dock waits for Finance; Tharushi's exam voucher is approved for October payroll; Liam's parking was paid by bank. Nimal's coffee receipt has an open receipt-agent suggestion.
- **Tests:** `pnpm --filter @amax/expenses test` (DB tests need `DATABASE_MIGRATE_URL`); screen: `pnpm --filter @amax/web build && pnpm --filter @amax/expenses e2e`.
