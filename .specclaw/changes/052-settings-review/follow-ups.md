### Escalation: the repeating finding and a proposed redesign

The two escalated findings are the third and fourth instances of one class: **a trace-store failure changes the outcome a person or the model is told.** Rounds 1 to 3 fixed it call site by call site (registry ok step, registry catch and refusal steps, confirm's replay steps). Rounds 4 and 5 then found the cancel step and the pending step. Patching more call sites won't close the class.

Proposed redesign (its own change, for the operator to approve):
1. Split the primitive in `packages/core/src/trace.ts`. `traceStep` becomes best-effort: it catches, logs with `logError` and never throws. A separate `traceStepOrFail` throws, and is allowed only where a step must exist before a write runs (today, only confirm's `run` step).
2. Remove the per-call-site `.catch` wrappers in `registry.ts` and `engine.ts`, so no new call site can forget them.
3. Add a lint or graph check: `traceStepOrFail` may be imported only by `packages/chat/src/engine.ts`.
4. In `runTurn`, write the pending step before pushing the card. Then one rule holds: a step that can't be written means nothing was proposed.
5. Add one table test that runs every `traceStep` call site (turn, pending, tool_call, decided confirm and cancel, mcp_call, job_run) with a failing trace store. It asserts that the outcome is unchanged, except for the single fail-closed site.
