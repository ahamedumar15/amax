- [minor B1] `apps/web/lib/in-app.ts:1` `inApp` rejects only `//` and `/\` straight after the leading slash. Browsers strip tab, CR and LF from URLs, so `/\t/evil.example` passes this check and becomes `//evil.example`, a different host. The test says "never another host" but covers only two spellings. Fix: reject any control character or whitespace too (e.g. `/^\/(?![/\\])[^\s\x00-\x1f]*$/`), or resolve with `new URL(url, base)` and compare the origin. Add those variants to the test.
