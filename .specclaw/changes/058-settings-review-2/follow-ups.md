## Open after the 5-round cap

Round 5 (the `--final` pass, Opus) returned exit 4 (escalate) with 2 blockers and 9 majors. The cap was
reached, so none of these was patched in this change. Each needs its own specclaw change.

### Escalated (exit 4): legal holds during erasure

- [blocker B1, escalated fp:689191f5726d] `packages/core/src/privacy/complete.ts:22` Erasure checks only whether the person's own ids or name are held; it ignores matter holds on the rows it is about to change. The purge honours them through `notHeld()`, `erase()` doesn't.
- [major B1] `packages/core/src/privacy/holds.ts:34` A person hold snapshots the person's ids when placed; ids gained later aren't held.
- **Proposed redesign:** one hold predicate for every write path. Holds store what they hold (`person`: the user id, re-resolved through `resolveSubject` on every read; `matter`: ids), `held()` returns a resolved `Match`, and every mutating path (purge, erasure by id, included name rows) builds its condition as `<rows> and notHeld(rule, held)`. Held rows the erasure skips become `kept: legal hold` items in the stored inventory. Test as a table over (person hold, matter hold on a project, matter hold on a row id, hold placed before or after the request) × (purge, erasure, included name row).

### Blockers

- [blocker B1] `modules/time/module.ts:39` Retention entries list personal columns in `fields` that aren't in `subject`/`subjectName` (`time.week.decided_by`, `time.entry.logged_by`, `presales.opportunity.lead_name`), so an approver's or logger's rows are neither erased, exported nor held. Proposed: every personal column in `fields` must also be a subject or subjectName column (enforce in `check-module-standard.mjs` and `defineModule`), with a test that erases an approver.

### Majors

- [major B1] `modules/allocations/module.ts:35` Erasure anonymises every field of a rule on any row whose subject matches, so erasing an employee wipes the PM's or approver's attribution and leaves the employee's own `person_id`. Proposed: per-column rules (each person column its own entry), or anonymise only the columns that matched.
- [major B3] `packages/core/src/privacy/name-rows.ts:24` Name-only rows past the 500-row listing limit are never listed, decided or acted on, and drop out of the inventory. Proposed: refuse to complete above the limit (or page), keeping unlisted rows as a review item.
- [major B3] `packages/core/src/privacy/package.ts:15` The access package caps each section and the audit list at 5000 rows with no record of truncation. Proposed: count first; page or mark the section truncated and refuse to complete.
- [major B1] `packages/core/src/rate-limit.ts:75` `countsAsPage` exempts any request with a prefetch or `next-action` header, which clients set freely; a GET with them still renders. Proposed: exempt `next-action` only on POST; count prefetches in their own bucket.
- [major B3] `packages/api/src/limits.ts:38` 429 counts are flushed by a 5 s timer; a frozen or recycled Vercel/Cloudflare instance drops them. Proposed: write in the request path with `after()`/`waitUntil`, or flush when a 429 is answered.
- [major B6] `packages/api/src/rest.ts:20` `PrivacyError` and other non-retryable domain refusals map to 500 `internal`. Proposed: map `retryable === false` errors to 400/409 with their message.
- [major B1] `packages/api/src/deliver.ts:38` A tenant with no `amax-webhook` signing secret gets every delivery counted as a receiver failure and the webhook switched off. Proposed: refuse to subscribe without the secret; treat `SecretNotConfiguredError` at delivery as a non-retryable setup error that doesn't count toward switch-off.
- [major B8] `packages/api/src/module.ts:13` GitHub and Graph calls have no signed timestamp, so their replay protection is the `api.inbound` dedupe row, which the purge deletes after the P90D floor. This is the third round on this class (round 1 dedupe key, round 2 floor). Proposed redesign: keep verified body hashes in a separate non-personal table with no time-based delete (or a very long one), outside the retention purge; `api.inbound` stays the personal-data log.
